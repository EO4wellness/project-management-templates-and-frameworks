# Issue Log — Checklist

A checklist to ensure your Issue Log is complete and actionable:

- Issue description is clear and factual  
- Impact is explicitly stated (schedule, scope, budget, quality, customer)  
- Severity is assigned consistently  
- Owner is identified and accountable  
- Status reflects current progress  
- Next steps are specific and time-bound  
- Target resolution date is realistic  
- Related risks or dependencies are cross-referenced  
- Issues older than 7 days have updated notes  
- High-severity issues have escalation paths documented  

Recommendation:  Update checklist items whenever retrospectives identify new previously unforeseen areas.
