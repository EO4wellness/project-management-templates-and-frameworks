# Decision Log — Template

The Decision Log records key choices made during the project, ensuring transparency, traceability, and alignment across teams.  
Use this log to track **what was decided**, **why**, **who approved it**, and **what changed** as a result.

---

## Decision Log Table

| ID | Date | Decision | Description / Context | Options Considered | Final Rationale | Approved By | Impact (Schedule/Scope/Budget/Quality) | Status (Pending/Approved/Rejected) |
|----|------|----------|-----------------------|---------------------|------------------|-------------|----------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| [#] | [YYYY-MM-DD] | [Short decision title] | [Why this decision was needed] | [Option A / Option B / etc.] | [Why the chosen option was selected] | [Name(s)] | [Impact] | [Status] |

---

## How to Use This Log

- **ID:** Sequential numbering (DEC-001, DEC-002, etc.)  
- **Decision:** Short, skimmable title  
- **Description / Context:** Why the decision was needed  
- **Options Considered:** Show alternatives to demonstrate due diligence  
- **Final Rationale:** Why the chosen option was selected  
- **Approved By:** Who made or authorized the decision  
- **Impact:** What changed as a result  
- **Status:** Pending → Approved → Rejected  

---

## Notes

- Update this log **as soon as a decision is made**, not later.  
- Link decisions to related **issues**, **risks**, **change requests**, or **dependencies**.  
- Use this log during retrospectives to analyze decision quality and patterns.
