# Decision Log — Checklist

A checklist to ensure your Decision Log entries are complete and useful:

- Decision title is short and clear  
- Context explains *why* the decision was needed  
- Options considered are listed (even briefly)  
- Final rationale is specific and defensible  
- Approved By: entries are clearly named. Person/Role 
- Impact is stated (schedule, scope, budget, quality)  
- Status is accurate  
- Entry is linked to related issues/risks/dependencies  
- No decisions are missing — especially those affecting timeline or scope  
- Decisions are written neutrally and factually
