# Issue Log — Template

The Issue Log tracks problems that are actively impacting the project or are likely to impact schedule, scope, budget, quality, or customer experience.  
Issues require **immediate attention**, **clear ownership**, and **documented next steps**.

---

## How to Use This Log

- **ID:** Sequential numbering (ISS-001, ISS-002, etc.)  
- **Issue Description:** Clear, concise, factual  
- **Impact:** What part of the project is affected  
- **Severity:** How serious the issue is  
- **Owner:** Person responsible for resolving the issue  
- **Status:** Open → In Progress → Resolved  
- **Next Steps:** Concrete actions, not vague statements  
- **Target Resolution Date:** When the issue should be resolved  

---

## Notes

- Update this log **daily** during execution.  
- Escalate issues that threaten milestones, budget, or customer experience.  
- Link issues to related **risks**, **dependencies**, and **change requests**.
